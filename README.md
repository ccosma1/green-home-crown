Fan game by a holder. Not affiliated with any token, studio, or official Bober project.

You ARE the summer king. Move. Strike. Crossbows hold the **capital**. The lane comes in packed. Scoop coins. Tap Next when you’re ready.

No install. No wallet. No login. No gacha. No PvP.

## Local

Run `START.bat` or open `index.html`.

## How to play

1. **FACE THE SUMMER**, pick a level.
2. **Move the King** — drag on the meadow or WASD / arrows. He auto-shoots nearby foes.
3. **Crossbows** only. Live pads sit near the **crown**. Place **50**, Heavy **90**, Royal Ballista **160**. The king is short-range support — towers do the killing.
4. **L1–2** place only between waves. **L3+** can place/upgrade during waves.
5. Enemies drain Crown HP only at the **pedestal**. Kite them through tower range.
6. **Royal Guard** once per level: all *placed* towers T3, and either ≥2 towers **or** the only live pad is already T3.
7. After the last wave, **scoop** leftover coins. **Next** flies remaining gold to the King, then the clear.

L1 start wood is **50** (one Crossbow). Kills drop **1–12** coins; income tapers after 280 dropped so the bank does not explode. Wood is for **place/upgrade crossbows only**. Mid/late waves need Heavy Crossbow / Ballista — T1-only will leak.
