# Green Home Crown mark

Metaphor: **a chunky three-point gold crown sitting on a sunny grass oval,
with a tiny cream heart on the crown band** — hold the crown, summer town.
This chapter is **day**. The field is a full-bleed summer sky, not the night
purple the siblings own.
At 32px the read is **gold crown + green oval on summer sky**, not a face,
not a hut, not logs, not a sword, not a slingshot, not a moss doorway.

Shape: **full-bleed square field** of summer sky `#87C6E8`. Primary
silhouette is a **stout 3-point crown** (gold `#F5C400`) sitting on a
**squat bright grass oval** (`#6B9E3A`). A **tiny cream heart** (`#F4E6C3`)
sits on the front of the crown's band. A **banner-red jewel** dots the
center point. Two small **ear-bumps** peek from behind the crown (ears
only — moss/fur knolls, **no** square chocolate-bar beaver FACE, no
buckteeth portrait). No moon, no sword, no stacked logs, no sling-V, no
chimney flame, no cream door slit, no snow hat, no text.

Palette:
- Summer sky `#87C6E8` (field — NOT night purple)
- Grass `#6B9E3A`
- Crown gold `#F5C400`
- Cream heart `#F4E6C3`
- Banner red `#C62828` (jewel)
- Fur/timber `#8B5A2B` / `#5C3A1A` (thin chunky outline support)
- Ink `#1A1028`

Forbidden (do not reuse):
- **Bober Yeet**: square chocolate-bar beaver FACE, yellow sling-V, giant buckteeth as the mark
- **Bober Dam Defense**: stacked horizontal capsule LOGS, crescent moon
- **Bober Frost Lodge**: snow-capped stick-lodge mound + hearth/chimney flame, timber door as hut
- **Bober Nightfall**: wooden sword in ice shard
- **Green Home Games hub**: moss haystack mound + cream door slit on night purple
- **Orion / grok-crypto-trader**: constellation, stars, deep-space navy, cyan rim, nebula, rounded-square + glow-circle
- **Lowlight**: iron lantern
- **ViaPass**: cream ticket
- CMC / MultiversX Bober coin art
- Text on the mark, wallet glyphs
- Night purple `#3A2A6A` as the field (siblings own night)

Hard rules:
- Original polygons only. No tracing coin art or sibling icons.
- Full-bleed square, not a squircle badge with an inner glow circle.
- Chunky outline, readable at 16px and 32px.
- Filename `green-home-crown.ico` so Windows icon cache cannot keep an old picture.
